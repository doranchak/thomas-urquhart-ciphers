A useful update to the source question raised above: Fable’s Distich mechanism is reproducible against the 1834 Proquiritation ordering, and a surviving 1653 copy at the National Library of Scotland (H.32.a.39) is documented with the Distich present. The BL/EEBO witness lacks it, so the discrepancy is copy-specific, not a disproof of the cipher.
We have also now obtained research scans from Glasgow’s physical 1652 The Jewel (Sp Coll Bi2-l.17), confirming that the Cyphral Octastich leaves are integral to that copy. The remaining task is coordinate-by-coordinate verification of the proposed Octastich solution.
The interesting lesson is that AI may have found a real structure, but historical cryptanalysis still needs copy-specific provenance and reproducibility.
