Method

Each line of the distich has 32 numbers, one for each of the 32 Proquiritations that precede it. For the i-th number n in a line, go to Proquiritation i, count to its n-th word, and take that word's first letter.
Word-counting conventions used
  1. Counting starts at the first word after the paragraph number; the closing signature initials (e.g. “K. F.”) are not counted.
  2. Words are separated by spaces and em-dashes; punctuation-only tokens (“;”) are not words.
  3. Hyphenated compounds count as two words (church-man, parol-breaking, country-men, twelve-month, time-server …); words merely split at a line end (progeni-tors) count as one.
  4. The Latin phrase hinc inde (Proquiritation 17) counts as a single word.
  5. Line 2, number 24 is read as 38, as printed on p. 417 of the scan.
